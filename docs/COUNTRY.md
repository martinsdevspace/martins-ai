# But there's a more important insight

I wouldn't actually tell the agent:

> "Find 100 companies in each country."

I'd make it search for **country × industry × pain point combinations**.

For example:

```text
UK × independent hotel groups × outdated booking systems
Canada × restaurant groups × fragmented ordering/CRM
Australia × property managers × manual tenant workflows
USA × local healthcare businesses × outdated customer portals
Ireland × professional services × manual client onboarding
Netherlands × e-commerce × custom automation opportunities
Germany × manufacturing SMEs × internal workflow modernization
UAE × hospitality × premium booking/customer experience
Spain × tourism businesses × direct-booking opportunities
New Zealand × tourism × reservation/CRM automation
```

That produces dramatically better leads than generic country searches.

# The real target: "digitally visible but digitally immature"

This should become one of the most important prospect filters.

The ideal Martins prospect often looks like:

> **Successful enough to pay, small enough to reach, digitally visible enough to research, but technically immature enough to need Martins.**

For example:

**Good prospect**

* 20 employees
* 4 restaurant locations
* Active Instagram
* 3,000+ reviews
* Good revenue indicators
* Website looks 5–8 years old
* No obvious customer portal
* Manual reservation/customer workflow
* Owner/operations manager identifiable
* Active social account
* Public business email

**Bad prospect**

* 1-person business with almost no revenue indicators
* Huge enterprise with an internal engineering department
* Startup already employing 30 developers
* Business with no obvious technology need
* Company impossible to reach
* Company that clearly doesn't have budget

# I would also add a "Response Probability" model

Your CRM shouldn't merely store:

> Opportunity Score: 87

It should separately estimate:

```text
Need Score              91
Martins Fit             94
Budget Potential        78
Decision-maker Access   87
Digital Pain            92
Responsiveness          81
Competition             55
Outreach Friction       23
────────────────────────────
Opportunity Score       87
Response Probability    74%
```

Then the agent can prioritize:

> **High opportunity + high probability of response**

rather than simply chasing companies with the biggest theoretical budgets.
